About TUNEVEN

Music. Mood. Moment.

TUNEVEN is a music-listening concept built around discovery. Instead of making users search endlessly, TUNEVEN connects music with artists, moods and experiences.

This foundation is designed so that individual musician pages and their song libraries can be added later without rebuilding the entire website.
